Sunken Garden

The UP Diliman Sunken Garden, officially named the General Antonio Luna Parade Grounds, is a five-hectare natural basin-shaped depression located on the eastern side of the University of the Philippines Diliman campus in Quezon City. It is a wide, grass-covered open space surrounded by large trees and key campus buildings.

Whether you want to jog, have a picnic, watch a sunset, or just sit under a tree and do nothing, the Sunken Garden delivers. It is the go-to spot for students and locals who need a break from the city without leaving campus. The open field feels massive, and the surrounding trees provide shade even on hot afternoons.

Details

Address UP Diliman Campus, Quezon City, Metro Manila
Opening Hours Open daily, 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM
Price Range Free entry
Best Time to Visit Late afternoon (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM) for cooler weather and sunset views
